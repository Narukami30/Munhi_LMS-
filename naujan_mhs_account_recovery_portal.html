Frequently Asked Questions

Account management guidelines for students, faculty, and administrative staff.

Where can I find my 12-digit Learner Reference Number (LRN)?

Your LRN is permanently printed on your official DepEd Form 138 (Report Card), Certificate of Enrollment, or previous school ID. It is a 12-digit number starting with '1098' or similar DepEd Division codes.

What should teachers and staff do if their DepEd Email is inaccessible?

Enter the username associated with your SERMS account. A verification code is sent only to the email address registered to that account. If you cannot access that email, submit a helpdesk ticket or contact the Registrar Office; staff cannot bypass verification codes.

How many failed attempts before an account is locked?

A verification code expires after 10 minutes and allows up to 5 attempts. Recovery requests are rate-limited; this process does not lock your sign-in account.
