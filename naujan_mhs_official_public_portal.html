1

Fill-out BEEF Online

Enter the learner and parent or guardian details requested in the online application form.

2

Document Review

3

Section Assignment

Any section assignment or external record verification is handled separately by the school.

4

Official Certification

Contact the school registrar for the next steps after your application status is updated.

Help & Citizen's Charter

Frequently Asked Questions

What information does the online application request?

The form requests the learner's LRN, name, grade, and contact details. Supporting-document uploads and external record verification are not available in this portal.

How do I check my application status?

Enter the tracking code shown after submitting your application. Status changes when the registrar updates the application in SERMS.

Does submitting an application mean I am enrolled?

No. A new application starts as Pending Review. The registrar must review and update its status before it is considered officially enrolled in SERMS.
